Perfectly positioned along the fairway, this recently renovated three-bedroom home combines refined contemporary style with comfort and effortless family living, enjoying uninterrupted views across the golf course towards the mountains beyond.

Designed for seamless indoor-outdoor living, the open-plan interiors lead onto a spacious timber deck and pool area — ideal for entertaining and relaxed everyday living. High exposed ceilings and expansive windows create a light-filled, welcoming atmosphere throughout, while the tasteful renovation enhances both functionality and modern appeal.

The home features three spacious bedrooms, two bathrooms, a study, formal and family lounges with a wood-burning fireplace, and a beautifully appointed modern kitchen with a separate scullery and laundry.

Additional features include:

• Double garage with golf cart storage

• Solar system with battery backup

• Main suite with dressing room and patio access

Positioned to maximise both outlook and privacy, the home offers a wonderful sense of space and tranquillity within Steenberg Golf Estate.

The purchase of the home includes an Exclusive Golf Club Membership, offering access to the esteemed Steenberg Golf Club, together with an additional golf playing right.

Come and experience the unique lifestyle and understated elegance of this beautifully home — contact Bernice to arrange your private viewing.
